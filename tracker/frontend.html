Methods, evidence, and machine-readable data

The public catalog is deterministically compiled from reviewed repository cards and pinned project snapshots. Claims preserve evidence category, source, observation date, review date, and limitations. The public evidence states are Verified, Officially reported, Registry reported, and Not established.

Scope

Resources may be NIH-operated or maintained, currently NIH-funded, historically or sustainably NIH-supported, ICO-associated, or external but relevant to NIH-funded research. Users can distinguish those categories.

Claim resolution

Reviewed identity evidence and source-bound claims are resolved deterministically. Related programs, components, portals, access systems, and repositories are not collapsed into identity without reviewed evidence.

Review lifecycle

High-stakes operational and policy claims are scheduled more frequently than stable descriptive fields. Every profile displays evidence dates and the next review due when established.

Limitations

Not established means the available evidence is insufficient. It does not prove that a capability, relationship, or service is absent.

NIH controlled-access and dbGaP context

CADR listing, operational availability, and study-level dbGaP association are separate claims. Public study-title, description, or collection text does not by itself establish that a resource is an external data source or access system for a particular study.

Human-data context

Human-data access context is a cautious derived facet combining source-bound organism terms with the resource-level access pathway. It does not determine consent, de-identification, privacy, or legal sensitivity for individual assets.

Operational and submission status

Operational availability, deposit eligibility, and access status are represented independently and carry their own evidence dates.

Publisher repository policies

Publisher mentions distinguish mandatory, recommended, preferred, illustrative, fallback, and directory-referral contexts where documented.
